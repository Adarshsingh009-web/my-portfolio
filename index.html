Startup Tips & Tricks

01

Build MVPs, Not Masterpieces

Click to expand details

Focus on coding core functionality first. Validate your ideas with real users using basic HTML, CSS, and JS before investing months in designing complex backend servers or database infrastructures.

Execution
02

Learn & Build in Public

Click to expand details

Document your programming and learning journey. Share screenshot updates, code learnings, and struggles on LinkedIn and GitHub. This naturally attracts co-founders, experienced mentors, and recruiters.

Marketing
03

Fall in Love with the Problem

Click to expand details

Do not fall in love with your own code. Fall in love with the user's pain point. If users struggle to use your MVP, pivot quickly and adjust your product strategy according to feedback.

Product Strategy
04

Talk to 10 Users Early

Click to expand details

Before coding a single feature, talk to at least 10 people in your target market. Verify if your solution solves a real, painful problem that they would actually care about or pay for.

Validation
05

Focus on Distribution First

Click to expand details

A great technical product will fail if nobody knows it exists. Plan how you will reach your first 100 users. Focus on marketing channels, startup networks, and student groups from day one.

Growth
06

Find Complementary Skills

Click to expand details

If you are the technical builder, find someone who excels at business, pitching, or design. A successful startup is a bridge between engineering excellence and commercial sales.

Teamwork
